## 5. Upcoming Architecture Topic: KL-ONE Roles & RoleFillers

Dr. Rainer has initiated an architectural discussion regarding `aim-rol` (Role) and the missing concept of `RoleFiller`:
1. **Current Issue:** In `packages/canvas/src/semanticRules.ts`, `SEMANTIC_RULES_MATRIX` contains **no rules for `rol`**. Therefore, users cannot connect edges to or from Role cards on the canvas.
2. **KL-ONE Triad (Brachman & Schmolze 1985):**
   - **Generic Concept:** e.g., `Contract` / `SignedContract` (`aim-cls` or `aim-obj`).
   - **Role / Slot Descriptor:** Structural constraint specifying Value Restriction and Cardinality (`aim-rol`).
   - **RoleFiller:** The individual entity filling that role (`aim-per` as RoleFiller).
3. **Proposed Connection Rules for Next Sprint:**
   ```typescript
   { source: 'obj', target: 'rol', relationship: 'binds', label: '«binds»' },
   { source: 'cls', target: 'rol', relationship: 'defines', label: '«defines»' },
   { source: 'per', target: 'rol', relationship: 'fills', label: '«fills»' },
   { source: 'act', target: 'rol', relationship: 'requires', label: '«requires»' },
   ```
Coordinate with Dr. Rainer and Vasco when formalizing this in the metamodel.
